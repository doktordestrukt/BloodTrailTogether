**BloodTrail Together v0.2.1** — fixes from the first real two-player session. Both players should update (run the new Setup and click **Repair / Update**).

### Fixed
- **Other player sunk into the floor** — their body now stands on the floor at the right height (head within a few cm of their real headset).
- **Always holding a gun** — the gun only shows when they actually hold something; otherwise they stand relaxed with empty hands.
- **Didn't die when shot** — damage is now a share of their real max health (250): a **headshot kills**, a chest shot takes half, a limb about a third. Before, it took 6+ hits.
- **Head movement** — their head now turns and tilts to look exactly where they look in their headset. Crouching in real life makes their body crouch.
- Shots from heavy rounds (12ga, .308, .50) hit harder; punches/melee do less.

### Still the same
Unity Relay join codes (no VPN / port forwarding), full-body players, shared NPCs (host's NPCs on both screens), shared sounds, map following.

Install: download **BloodTrailTogether-Setup-v0.2.1.exe** below, run it, Install / Repair, launch BloodTrail through Steam, press **F9**.
