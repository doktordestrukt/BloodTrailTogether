# BloodTrail Together 0.3.0

Both players must update: the network protocol changed (v3) and 0.2.x cannot connect to 0.3.0.

## Fixed
- **Crossed / spinning legs**: the other player's body now has a look direction, so the game's animation faces where they look, strafes and backpedals, and turns on the spot. Planted-foot twisting is fixed.
- **Head and arms**: the head and neck follow the headset exactly; each arm reaches its own controller (two-bone IK).
- **Floating blood**: NPC hits are sent relative to the bone that was hit, so wounds land on the same spot on both screens.
- **Falling over after one shot**: the other player's body never ragdolls while they are alive.
- **Lag and frame rate**: the expensive per-frame object scans are gone, and the relay now uses the nearest region (about 60 ms instead of about 220 ms in Europe).
- **Gunshots in your ear**: the other player's gunshots now play in 3D using the game's own gunshot attenuation (direction and distance falloff).
- Players can no longer grab each other.

## Added
- **Held weapons**: every visible part of what the other player holds (any gun, magazine, attachments, knife) is shown in the right hand, at the right angle, including reloads.
- **Head lamp and night vision** are synced.
- **Grabbing and throwing**: dragging NPC bodies and throwing them, plus map objects (bottles, boxes, guns lying in the level), are synced.
- **NPC sync** for both players (host-authoritative), including corpses.

## Not yet tested in VR
Grabbing and throwing, thrown props, night vision and the multi-part weapon display were built and installed, but have not been tested in a real two-player VR session. Please report problems on the Issues page.
