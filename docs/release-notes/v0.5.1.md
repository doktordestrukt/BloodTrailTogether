# BloodTrail Together 0.5.1

## Fixed
- **Arms**: when a controller isn't tracked (switched off, asleep, not found yet), that arm now hangs relaxed at the player's side instead of the NPC's clasped-hands pose. Tracked arms still follow their controllers freely.
- **Crash fix**: the game could crash when the other player held a gun your game hadn't loaded yet, because the mod tried to load the gun model from disk at runtime. Guns are now drawn only from models already in memory (normally every gun), and their type is checked first.
- **Sound sharing** is always switched on at start.
- The F9 window says "IN A GAME" instead of "HOSTING..." when you joined someone else's game.

## Tested
Tested in the game with a simulated second player:
- Arms hang at the sides when controllers aren't tracked, and reach the controllers when they are.
- Held gun shown in the hand, 7 cm from the hand bone.
- Name tag and health bar shown.
- Gunshots with 3D sound and bullet impacts, with no crash.

Not yet tested in a real two-player VR session.
