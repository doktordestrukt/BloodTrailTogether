# BloodTrail Together 0.5.0

Includes everything from 0.4.0, which was never published separately. 0.3.x players can still join, but won't see the new effects. Updating is recommended for both players.

## New in 0.5.0
- **Finger poses**: the other player's fingers wrap around whatever they hold (index finger on the trigger), make a fist while gripping, and relax otherwise.
- **Muzzle flashes** on every shared gunshot, including NPC shots aimed at you.
- **Bullet impacts**: you see where the other player's bullets land, with the game's own bullet-hole effects for wood, metal, tile, plaster, brick, dirt, grass, cloth and concrete.
- **Hologram watch menu**: tap your left wrist with your right hand. It offers host, quick join and leave, plus switches for name tags, health bars, bullet effects and sharing your sounds. Settings are remembered.

## New in 0.4.0
- **Server list**: click HOST GAME and others see your game in their list and join with one click. No more codes.
- **Redesigned, animated F9 window.**
- **Mixed versions**: players on compatible versions can join each other. Otherwise you get a clear "update needed" message and an UPDATE button.
- **NPCs attack both players**: NPCs shoot at whichever player is closer.
- **In VR**: a health bar over the other player, a wrist status panel, and a warning when the connection gets weak.
- **Installer icon.**

## Not yet tested in VR
Everything in 0.4.0 and 0.5.0 was built and installed without a two-player VR session. Please report problems on the Issues page.
