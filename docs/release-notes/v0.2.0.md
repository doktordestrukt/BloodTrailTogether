**BloodTrail Together v0.2.0** — two-player PCVR multiplayer for BloodTrail. Both players need this version.

### Install (both players)
1. Download **BloodTrailTogether-Setup-v0.2.0.exe** below and run it. It finds BloodTrail in your Steam libraries and adds UE4SS if you don't have it (your other mods are kept).
2. Click **Install** (or **Repair / Update** if you had v0.1.0), then start BloodTrail through Steam.
3. Press **F9** for the BloodTrail Together window on your desktop.
4. Host: **Create Session** and send your friend the code. Friend: type the code and **Join Session**.
5. The friend is moved to the host's map automatically. Play in any Sandbox or Arcade map.

Windows SmartScreen may warn because the installer is not code-signed (More info → Run anyway). Checksums: `SHA256SUMS.txt`.

### New in 0.2.0
- **Full-body players**: the other player is shown as the game's human character (with name tag and ping), walking where they walk. You keep your own gloves.
- **Shoot each other**: your bullets hit their body with the game's normal gore, and the damage is applied to them for real. When they die, their body ragdolls on your screen; it comes back when they respawn. Punches/melee count when your hand actually connects.
- **Shared NPCs**: both players see the host's NPCs in the same places. Shots from either player hit them with gore on both screens, and they die for both. (The friend's game removes its own NPCs and shows the host's.) Turn off with `NpcSync=0` in config.ini if needed.
- UE4SS is installed automatically when missing.

### Still the same
- Online play through Unity Relay with a short join code — no VPN, no port forwarding.
- Shared gunshot/hand sounds, following the host between maps, simulated player for testing alone.

### Known limitations
- Shared NPCs: enemies attack the host's player normally; attacks on the friend are not shared yet. Dead bodies may land in slightly different poses on each screen.
- The other player's arms follow their controllers only roughly; their outfit is random.
- Tested with BloodTrail build 18598125.

Bug reports: please attach `BTVR\Binaries\Win64\ue4ss\UE4SS.log` and `ue4ss\Mods\BloodTrailTogether\BloodTrailTogether.log`.
