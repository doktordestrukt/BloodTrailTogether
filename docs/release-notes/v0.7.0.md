# BloodTrail Together 0.7.0

## New
- **Melee NPCs go for the joining player too.** Unarmed and melee NPCs chase whichever player is closer. When it's the joining player, the NPC walks up to them and swings about every 1.5 s. Hits do punch or club damage, and the joining player's copy of the NPC turns to face them.
- **Scoreboard on the watch.** Kills (NPCs and the other player), headshots and deaths for both players. It's on the watch status hologram and in the watch menu. A kill counts for whoever hit that NPC last, within 5 seconds of its death. Each NPC kill also pops a "Kill (n)" note.
- **Spectate while dead.** When you die and your friend is alive, your view moves smoothly to a camera behind and above them and follows them gently. It returns to you when you respawn. You can switch it off on the watch ("SPECTATE WHEN DEAD").

## Doors and switches
BloodTrail has no door or switch blueprints; its levels don't use them. Doors and objects you can grab are physics objects, and those already move in both games through the grab-and-throw sync.

## Tested
Tested in the game as host for 90 seconds, with two live AI NPCs and the simulated friend being shot dead and respawning repeatedly, with no errors. Not tested yet: a melee NPC chasing the joining player, spectating (it needs you to die in VR), and a real two-player VR session.
