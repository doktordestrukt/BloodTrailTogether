# BloodTrail Together 0.8.0

## Character customisation
- A **CHARACTER** button in the F9 window opens an animated character panel.
- **Live 3D preview:** the game renders your character on a lit red stage, and the window shows it live. Drag to rotate it; when you leave it alone it turns slowly on its own.
- **Choose your look** from the game's own clothing:
  - Top: hoodie, tank top or long-sleeve shirt, 8–12 colours each.
  - Bottom: cargo shorts or jeans, with colours.
  - Sneakers in 4 colours.
  - Beanie or cap, 11–14 colours.
  - Hair.
  - Every slot can also be "None".
- **New characters** start in plain base clothes: a white tank top, tan cargo shorts and hair.
- **Outfits:** name an outfit and press SAVE. Click a saved outfit to wear it, or ✕ to delete it.
- **Name effects:** Classic, Rainbow, Sunset, Fire, Ice, Gold, Toxic, Pulse, Glitch and Wave. Your name in the panel shows the effect live.
- **Synced:** your look and name effect are sent to the other player, whose game dresses your body to match and shows your name letter by letter with the effect.

## Third person
A **3RD PERSON** button switches to an over-the-right-shoulder view: a dressed copy of you walks where you walk, and the camera sits behind your right shoulder (it still follows your head in VR). **Not yet tested in VR**: the desktop test has no player character. Please report how it feels.

## Arms
The other player's arms follow their controllers in the joining player's game and on maps without NPCs. On the **host with live AI NPCs**, the body still uses the game's own arm animation. I tested two other ways in this version and both crashed the game there, so the stable mode stays.

## Tested

- The other player dressed in a chosen outfit, with an animated name effect.
- 100 seconds as host with live AI NPCs, no crash.
