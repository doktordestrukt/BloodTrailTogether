# BloodTrail Together 0.6.1

Fixes from the first play test.

## Fixed
- **Free movement**: while walking, the other player's legs go where they move, and their upper body and head turn toward where they look. Before, the body always turned to face the look direction. Walking backwards still backpedals, and standing still still turns on the spot.
- **No more flinging**: bullet hit reactions no longer throw the other player's body around while they're alive. Wounds and blood still show.
- **Subtitles**: the old captions were invented sentences that often didn't match what the NPC said. Now a caption shows the actual words when the recording's file name contains them; otherwise it describes the line, for example "[calls for help]" or "[begs for his life]".
- **Watch**: much more forgiving. Tapping anywhere near your left wrist or controller with your right hand opens the menu, the status hologram shows from more angles, and it sits closer to the wrist.

## Simulated player ("Mirror me" / "Walk around")
- It now holds what you hold (in the mirrored hand).
- It takes damage, dies, and respawns after 6 seconds, like a real player.
- Its echoed gunshots now come from its gun, not its head.
- The mirror copies you through an imaginary mirror, so it can walk through walls. That only happens with the test bot, not with a real player.

## Tested
In the game with the simulated player walking in a circle: body faced the walking direction while the head looked elsewhere; two shots killed it, then it respawned, repeatedly, with no errors. Not yet tested in a real two-player VR session.
