# BloodTrail Together 0.6.0

## Crash fix (important, affects 0.3.0–0.5.1)
On the **host**, with live AI NPCs on the map, the game crashed within about a minute of the other player joining. The cause was the extra mesh the mod uses to drive the other player's arms and head. On the host, while AI NPCs are around, the other player's body now uses the game's own animation and head look-at instead. Everywhere else the full arm and head tracking stays, including the joining player's game and maps without NPCs.

## Smarter, more human NPCs (single-player and multiplayer)
- **Hearing:** NPCs hear **both** players. The other player's gunshots and noises go to the game's AI hearing, so NPCs investigate them too.
- **Out of ammo:** an NPC that stays out of ammo walks to the nearest dead body and takes its ammo.
- **Reading the fight:** badly hurt, or seeing two friends die nearby with a player close, NPCs either run for it or give up (the game's own surrender / beg-for-life behaviour).
- **Single-player:** all of this works without multiplayer too.

## NPC subtitles
- When an NPC speaks, a caption appears above its head. It's white text with a black shadow behind it, so it reads on light and dark backgrounds. It pops in, holds, and shrinks away.
- The game has no subtitle text for these recordings, so captions come from each line's type (and the line's own words where the file name contains them).
- The joining player now hears the host's NPCs speak too.
- You can switch subtitles on or off in the watch menu.

## Also
- Notes in front of your face animate in and out.
- Sound sharing is always on at start.

## Tested
Tested in the game as host, with two live AI NPCs and a simulated second player for two minutes, with no crash. Earlier 0.5.1 tests covered the joining player's game. Not yet tested in a real two-player VR session.
